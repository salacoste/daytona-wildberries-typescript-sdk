## Context

### Background

Orders FBW (Fulfillment by Wildberries) is a critical business module for sellers who use Wildberries' warehouse infrastructure for order fulfillment. Unlike FBS (Fulfillment by Seller) where sellers manage their own warehouses, FBW sellers ship products to WB warehouses and WB handles the final delivery to customers.

**Key FBW Operations**:
- **Warehouse Management**: List available warehouses, get warehouse details
- **Supply Creation**: Create supplies (shipments to WB warehouses)
- **Supply Tracking**: Monitor supply status throughout the process
- **Barcode Management**: Generate and manage barcodes for supplies
- **Supply Closure**: Close supplies and initiate shipment

### Technical Context

**API Documentation**: `wildberries_api_doc/07-orders-fbw.yaml`

**Base URLs** (from Swagger):
- `https://supplies-api.wildberries.ru` - Supply management operations
- `https://marketplace-api.wildberries.ru` - Warehouse operations

**Key OpenAPI Paths** (to be verified from Swagger):
```yaml
/api/v1/warehouses - List WB warehouses
/api/v1/supply - Supply CRUD operations
/api/v1/supply/{supplyId} - Supply details
/api/v1/supply/{supplyId}/barcode - Barcode generation
/api/v1/supply/{supplyId}/close - Supply closure
```

**Rate Limits** (to be extracted and verified from Swagger):
- Most operations: ~1 request per second
- Supply creation: ~3 requests per minute with intervals
- Barcode generation: ~5 requests per minute
