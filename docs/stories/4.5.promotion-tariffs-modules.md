## Story

**As a** Wildberries seller managing marketing campaigns and pricing strategy,
**I want** to create and manage advertising campaigns, control budgets, and access commission/tariff information through the SDK,
**so that** I can optimize product visibility, control advertising spend, and understand marketplace costs.

---

## Context

### Background

This story combines two related modules for seller business operations:

**Promotion Module** (MEDIUM-HIGH complexity): Enables sellers to create and manage advertising campaigns on the Wildberries platform, including campaign creation, bid management, budget control, and performance statistics. Advertising is critical for product visibility and sales growth in the competitive marketplace.

**Tariffs Module** (LOW complexity): Provides read-only access to marketplace tariff information including commission rates, box storage costs, pallet costs, and return handling fees. Essential for profit margin calculations and pricing strategies.

**Key Operations**:
- **Campaign Management**: Create, list, update, start/pause/stop advertising campaigns
- **Bidding System**: Set and manage bids for ad placements (search, catalog, product card)
- **Budget Control**: Manage campaign finances and spending limits
- **Statistics**: Track campaign performance and ROI
- **Tariff Information**: Access commission and fee structures for cost calculations
